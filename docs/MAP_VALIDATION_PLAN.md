# Map validation and verified repairs: current plan

This is the authoritative plan and the only active milestone checklist for the
map-validation programme approved on 22 October 2026. Research receipts
support this plan; they do not replace it or silently change its scope.

**Intended outcome:** the launcher validates imports against an exact game build,
explains findings and missing coverage, and applies only independently verified,
feature-preserving fixes to separate editions. The same inspection service must
work on newly customized map directories without requiring import or activation.

**Current step:** A2 in the Austrian Monarchy section below, tracked in
[issue #251](https://github.com/Craig-Franklin/Definitive-SMR-Launcher-Apple-Silicon/issues/251).

no gameplay input if the map loads. This requires a fresh finite job; the earlier
unused automated job remains retired. Current authority does not permit taking
over a user-owned game or restoring a profile underneath one. Each attempt needs
one explicit question, setup, memory/time bounds and retained evidence before
another attempt is proposed. The original M0–M4 and fresh-install
F1–F5 programmes remain
complete at their recorded scopes. The historical reset authority below does
not authorize new deletion. Original packages, maps, profiles and saves remain
preserved. Other maps and historical recipes do not inherit repair eligibility.

**Retained M2 limits:** a separate one-key Liverpool and Manchester 2
diagnostic now loads a visible world; its original reached the six-GiB guard with

## Repair promotion rules

A confirmed error and a justified correction are different conclusions. A repair
must have exact applicability, independently supported preconditions, an
unambiguous transformation, source/output identities and a complete change record.
Renaming identities must preserve dependent references and intended behavior.
Do not silently delete events, effects, industries, objectives or other authored
features. Successful load or save/reopen alone does not prove feature preservation.

Evidence stays separate: mechanical checks, conditional static findings, synthetic
controls, native causal observations, feature preservation and runtime acceptance.
Unknown builds and changed inputs invalidate applicable conclusions. No warning
or inspection success is a universal compatibility certificate.
